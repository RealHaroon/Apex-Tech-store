About us

Two students, one problem worth solving.

Apex Tech Store started from a simple frustration: genuine software and real tech support shouldn't be this hard to find.

Our story

We're two BS Computer Science students at the University of Sindh who kept running into the same problem — classmates and small shop owners around us either paid too much for software they didn't understand, or turned to pirated copies that came with malware, no support, and constant activation issues.

Apex Tech Store is our answer: a straightforward, trustworthy place to buy genuine software, get it properly set up, learn how to actually use it, and get your hardware fixed when it breaks — plus the everyday accessories that go with all of it.

What we stand for

Genuine, always

No pirated keys or shared accounts — everything we sell is properly licensed.

Real support

We stay reachable after the sale, not just until payment clears.

Fair pricing

Student and regional pricing passed through honestly, not marked up.

Skills that last

We'd rather teach you than keep you dependent on us.
